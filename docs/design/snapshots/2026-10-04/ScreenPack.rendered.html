P03Discussion[D01m]

Oil vs. hardwax on a walnut desk top?

mara_k · 4 replies · Active 10m
#1
Sanded to 220. It’s a daily desk: coffee mugs and a laptop. Rubbing oil looks great, but I’m worried about rings. Would hardwax oil hold up better, or should I go straight to a film finish?
#2
Hardwax oil is easier to spot-repair. Two thin coats, buff off the excess, and give it a full week before the mugs come back.
#3
Test both on an offcut from the same board. Walnut can go darker under oil than you expect.
#4
If the laptop always sits in one place, a film finish resists the heat better. Hardwax will dull there eventually.
#5
Whatever you pick, try it on the underside first.
