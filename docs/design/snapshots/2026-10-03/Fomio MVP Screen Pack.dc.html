Accessibility settings and iPad

A01–A03 are the live component with a setting applied; you can keep navigating inside them. A04–A05 are static iPad compositions. Light/dark pairs are in section 0. None of this is evidence of native behaviour; see the verification notes.

A01Discussion · Reduce Transparency[D01m]
SIMGlass controls become opaque Fomio surfaces (#F4F3F7 light, #1E1D24 dark) with a hairline edge; Reply becomes solid #5B3FD6. The scroll edge stops blurring. Natively the system does this when Reduce Transparency is on.
A02Home · dark · Increase Contrast + Reduce Transparency[H01m]
SIMSecondary text lifts to #CFCDD8, separators to #5A5863, accent to #BFAFFF, and every control gets a 1 px #8E8C98 border so its shape does not rely on material.
A03Discussion · Larger Text[AX01]
ESTAll app text scales together by about 135% (one representative Dynamic Type step). Lines wrap, rows grow, nothing truncates except the inline nav title. Tab labels stay fixed and rely on the system Large Content Viewer. The ~200% accessibility-size checks are in section 4.
