Composer state model. Six independent axes, each with its own indicator: intent (new discussion · reply · quote · edit · resume) × presentation (full screen · sheet · iPad form) × editor mode (unresolved) × draft status (not saved · saving · saved on this device · saved to your account · couldn't save) × per-upload status (queued · uploading % · done · failed) × submission status (idle · sending · published · pending review · rejected · unconfirmed). Resize, rotation and backgrounding never reset any axis. No idempotency or guaranteed offline sync is promised.
SAMPLE-ONLY marks a link to a generic frame whose topic or tab differs from the origin. This is a linked frame board: it doesn't prove editable inputs, draft recovery, tab-stack persistence or device accessibility.
